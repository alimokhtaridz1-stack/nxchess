# NX Chess website plan

## Goal

Introduce NX Chess as a chess education brand, make the learning path understandable, and make contacting the team easy.

The website should feel like people welcoming visitors to learn chess: calm, clear, thoughtful, and approachable.

## Scope

Four public pages: Home, Curriculum, About, and Contact.
A shared header and footer connect them. Visitors can read everything without signing in.

Working audience assumption: learners and parents considering chess instruction. Confirm actual ages served, experience levels, location, languages, and online or in-person availability before final copy.

## Visitor journeys

- New visitor: Home → Curriculum → Contact.
- Parent: Home → About → Curriculum → Contact.
- Returning visitor: any page → visible Contact link → preferred verified channel.

The main outcome is an inquiry. Do not create an enrollment or account workflow.

## Page structure

### Home — understand NX Chess quickly

1. Compact header: brand, Curriculum, About, Contact.
2. Hero: a specific introduction, one authentic image, primary "Explore the curriculum" link, secondary "Talk to us" link.
3. A brief teaching approach: how lessons help learners think, practice, and reflect.
4. Curriculum preview: a connected sequence of learning stages, linking to the full page.
5. A personal coach or team introduction using verified information.
6. A short contact invitation.
7. Footer with navigation and verified contact channels.

Avoid adding empty testimonials, unsupported credentials, or statistics for visual filler.

### Curriculum — see what learning involves

1. Brief explanation of how the learning path works.
2. A linked list of stages for quick navigation.
3. Stage details: who it is for, topics, example practice, and observable outcomes.
4. Explain that placement is discussed with the team; do not add a quiz or automated assessment.
5. Contact invitation: "Not sure where to start? Talk to us."

Use the proposed structure in CONTENT.md until the real curriculum is supplied.

### About — meet the people and approach

1. The actual NX Chess story, once supplied.
2. Coach or team profiles with confirmed credentials only.
3. Teaching approach, supported by concrete examples.
4. Approved images of the people or teaching environment.
5. Link to curriculum and contact.

### Contact — make reaching us effortless

1. Friendly introduction: "Ask about lessons or the curriculum."
2. Verified WhatsApp, email, phone, or social channels; display only those supplied.
3. Real location, lesson format, and contact hours if confirmed.
4. Optional short FAQ using owner-confirmed answers.

No form by default. A visitor should know whether a link opens email, WhatsApp, or a telephone call.

## Phases after implementation is requested

1. **Content foundation:** confirm brand details, audience, curriculum, and at least one contact channel.
2. **Design foundation:** prepare visual styling and a Home page preview.
3. **Public pages:** complete Curriculum, About, and Contact with shared navigation.
4. **Quality review:** check mobile layouts, accessibility, content accuracy, performance, and contact actions.
5. **Launch:** connect the approved domain and hosting destination when publishing is authorized.

## Definition of done

All four pages communicate their purpose clearly, the curriculum is readable without an account, and visitors can reach NX Chess through working verified links. Layouts work on phones and desktops. No unconfirmed claims or draft placeholders are published.

## Decisions awaiting the owner

- Who is taught: children, adults, beginners, experienced players?
- Which curriculum stages and topics are actually offered?
- Who teaches, and what profile details can be published?
- Which contact channels should appear?
- Online, in person, or both; which location and languages?
- Existing logo, colors, real photography, and preferred visual references?
- Domain and hosting preference, when launch is requested?

These questions do not authorize adding services or features.
