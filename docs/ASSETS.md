# Asset provenance

## Chess photography

- Subject: close-up of wooden chess pieces on a board.
- Source: https://www.pexels.com/photo/brown-and-black-wooden-chess-piece-163427/
- Source author: Pixabay.
- Source page labels the asset free to use (CC0); checked during implementation.
- Served from the source-provided images.pexels.com image URL with responsive compression parameters in src/content.mjs.
- Used as an illustrative chess photograph, not a photograph of NX Chess people or premises. Visible captions make that distinction.
- Attribution is retained in the footer even though the source page lists CC0.
- No unrelated portraits, facilities, certificates, or testimonials have been added.

## Typography and mark

- DM Sans and Libre Caslon Display are loaded through Google Fonts, with Arial and Georgia fallbacks.
- The brand and favicon use a Unicode knight glyph; rendering may vary slightly by system font.
- Chess glyphs elsewhere are decorative and hidden from assistive technology.

External image and font delivery require internet access. Consider self-hosting appropriately licensed files before launch if eliminating those external requests is important. Image and font loading were checked in the browser during implementation.
